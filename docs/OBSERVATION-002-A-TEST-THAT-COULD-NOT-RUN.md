# Observation 002 — A preregistered test that could not run, twice

**Status: observational field study. Hand-authored, not harness-emitted.**

No adapter in this repository reproduces this. The dataset is public and the analysis scripts are
described in full, so the computation can be repeated; the result being reported is a **null**, and
nulls are the ones most worth being able to check.

Companion to [Observation 001](OBSERVATION-001-ABSTENTION-IS-NOT-SAFETY.md). They are the same
finding seen from two directions: an apparatus that returns a clean answer it could never have
failed to return.

## Summary

A research hypothesis was generated by a machine, preregistered by hand, and tested against a public
NASA dataset. **It was never tested.** Two attempts both terminated at a validity precondition
frozen in advance: the labels needed to score the test survive in too few of the dataset's
specimens.

No detector was fitted. No fold was scored. No margin was compared.

**The hypothesis is untested, not refuted.** It is released below for anyone to pick up.

The reportable content is the process: what the preregistration caught, what it cost to honour, and
the three ways the null could have been converted into a result by choices that would each have
looked reasonable in a paper.

## Provenance of the hypothesis

The question was not chosen by a person. It was produced by an autonomous literature-scanning
engine, from four sentences mined out of four unrelated published papers, and it disclaims itself in
its own record: `evidence_roots_minted: 0`, `scientific_claim: false`, `novelty_established: false`,
`navigation_hypothesis_only: true`, and explicitly `leap_attention_is_not_confidence: true`.

That self-disclaiming structure is what made it safe to preregister. The engine did not assert a
finding; it proposed a direction and priced the cost of checking it.

## The hypothesis, released

Anyone may take this. It is a navigation hypothesis, not a claim.

> **Rolling-centroid early warning for state-aware impact/thermal protection**
>
> **Bottleneck.** Coupled impact and thermal protection remains difficult to sustain because hidden,
> history-dependent damage states are not visible early enough for selective intervention.
>
> **Suspected mechanism.** Rolling local prototypes track drifting multimodal sensor clusters and
> create nonlinear operational-state boundaries; those boundaries expose impending damage transitions
> early enough to trigger selective protective actions that conserve mechanical and thermal reserves.
>
> **Weakest link (its own words).** That measurable precursor clusters exist early enough, and remain
> invariant enough across loading histories, to support useful intervention rather than retrospective
> labeling.
>
> **Cheapest falsifier.** On archived or benchtop coupon data, compare a rolling-centroid regime
> detector with linear and random-label controls using leave-one-loading-history-out validation;
> reject the bridge if transition lead time and balanced accuracy do not exceed preregistered margins.

Its four source claims, each a single sentence from a published paper:

| claim | source |
|---|---|
| synergistic impact/thermal coupling protection remains a challenge | `10.1002/adma.202522056` |
| a rolling window of centroids produces nonlinear decision boundaries without kernel functions | `10.2139/ssrn.4729393` |
| composite stiffness degrades with increasing N, indicating microcracking | `10.2139/ssrn.3787881` |
| stiffness degrades dramatically within ~20,000 cycles, then remains nearly constant | `10.2139/ssrn.3787881` |
| damage can be located in a strong-noise environment | `10.2139/ssrn.4003242` |

The third and fourth are the load-bearing pair: they say degradation is **not** a smooth trend but a
transition followed by a plateau. A regime boundary is only worth detecting if regimes exist.

## What was preregistered

Frozen before the dataset was downloaded: leave-one-layup-out splits; normalisation fitted inside
training folds only; no non-causal filtering; **mandatory linear and random-label controls**; a 10%
stiffness-drop threshold; and named margins the premise had demanded but not specified — ≥0.15
balanced accuracy over the random-label control, ≥0.05 over the linear control, and a median
precursor lead time whose bootstrap CI excludes zero.

Also frozen: the conditions under which the run must **declare itself non-informative rather than
report a result** — fewer than three surviving loading histories, or ground truth too sparse to
locate a transition.

That last clause is the entire content of this observation.

## What happened

**Dataset.** NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence, *Composites*, 4,638,330,974 bytes,
`sha256:5b0a4e71069f4133aeeb0b733ad66f3af9e17015a6f455daa74bc0939733e1e4`. Three layups, 13
specimens, 1,348 inspections, 252 actuator→sensor lamb-wave paths each at 1.2 MHz.

**The signals are complete.** Feature extraction succeeded on 1,348 of 1,348 files.

**The labels are not.** The stiffness ground truth is sparse:

| attempt | ground truth | usable loading histories | required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the dataset's `stiffness_degradation` field | **1** | 3 |
| 2 (amendment) | stiffness re-derived from raw strain gauges | **2** | 3 |

Both non-informative. The amendment was written and frozen before it ran, declared itself the second
and final attempt before its result was known, and changed **only** the ground-truth source — the
threshold, the split, the controls and the margins were untouched, so that a favourable outcome could
not have been attributed to two changes at once.

## What honouring the preregistration cost

Three rescues were available at the point of failure. Each is a decision a reviewer would probably
wave through. Each is recorded here as declined.

1. **Move the threshold from 10% to 8%.** This promotes exactly one specimen — the one sitting at
   0.084 — and yields a second layup. A two-point move to precisely the value that saves the study.
2. **Switch from leave-one-layup-out to leave-one-specimen-out.** Manufactures folds immediately, and
   destroys the thing being tested: the hypothesis requires gains to survive *held-out loading
   histories*, and two specimens of one layup share theirs.
3. **Keep fixing the reader until it passes.** The most insidious, because it looks like diligence.

The protection against (3) was a stopping rule fixed before the result: implement the file formats,
run once, report whatever comes out — not "keep going until three layups appear."

## Two of my own defects that nearly became findings

Both were caught by checking a null before publishing it, which is the habit this observation is
really about.

1. **A resolver bug produced a false null.** A spreadsheet cell may name several strain gauges at
   once, comma-separated. The resolver concatenated them into one unmatchable key and silently
   discarded the *only* baseline reading of the best-instrumented specimen. That specimen went from
   "unusable, no baseline" to 19 readings across 19 levels after a one-line fix. The pre-fix output
   was a non-informative verdict caused entirely by my own code, and it looked exactly like a
   non-informative verdict caused by the data.
2. **A second file format was invisible.** Strain data is stored three ways: MATLAB `.mat`,
   extension-less tab-separated text, and an undocumented length-prefixed binary. A `*.mat` glob saw
   only the first, so six specimens were written off as having no strain data at all. Writing and
   validating a text reader — **44/44 against matched `.mat` files, worst channel deviation
   4.93×10⁻⁵ from |corr| = 1** — moved the corpus from one usable layup to two.

A null that is really a bug is indistinguishable from a real null unless someone goes looking.

## The format that was left unparsed, and why

231 of 594 strain files are in the undocumented binary format. It was not parsed.

**It cannot be validated.** Checked explicitly: **zero** binary files have a `.mat` counterpart
naming the same measurement — the format appears only in the six specimens that have no `.mat` files
at all. There is no reference output to check a parser against, and those specimens are precisely the
ones that decide whether the experiment runs.

An unvalidated parser for an undocumented layout produces numbers that look entirely reasonable.
Writing one would have converted an honest *cannot test* into a possibly-false *tested*.

## A finding about the dataset, offered separately

This is reportable on its own and does not depend on the hypothesis.

In this widely-used corpus, the stiffness ground truth is populated to a usable degree in **two
specimens, in two different layups**, and stored in an unparseable format for roughly a third of the
strain files. The lamb-wave signal data is complete and excellent throughout.

**Any published result using this corpus for cross-layup supervised damage-regime classification is
resting on a very small number of labelled specimens, and should state which.**

## A caveat recorded before it became convenient

The strain-derived stiffness proxy reports 0.961 maximum degradation — a 96% stiffness loss — for a
specimen whose own `stiffness_degradation` field peaks at 0.128. The proxy is very likely not
measuring modulus.

This was written down when it first appeared, not after the null was known. If the proxy is wrong,
the frozen method was wrong, and that is a finding about the amendment rather than a licence to
substitute a better proxy and re-run.

## Why this sits beside Observation 001

Observation 001: a model abstained in 95.4% of invocations and fabricated zero citations, which reads
as a safety result until you notice that 77% of those invocations supplied an empty evidence set. The
screen was structurally incapable of detecting the thing it screened for.

Observation 002: a hypothesis test terminated because the stratum it depends on contains two
instances where it needs three. The test was structurally incapable of moving.

**Same failure, opposite visibility.** The first returns a clean number and is easy to publish. The
second returns nothing and is easy to rescue. Both are Effect Reachability: an apparatus whose
endpoint depends on a stratum that is empty or nearly so.

The asymmetry is the practical lesson. Nobody is tempted to publish a null. Everybody is tempted to
publish 95.4%.

## Reproduction

Public dataset, digest above. The pipeline: extract per-path RMS damage indices from each inspection
(label-independent, so it cannot influence any design choice); derive stiffness from load and strain;
audit usable specimens against the frozen criteria; **stop if fewer than three layups survive**.

The audit is the whole experiment as run. There is no model to reproduce, because none was fitted.
